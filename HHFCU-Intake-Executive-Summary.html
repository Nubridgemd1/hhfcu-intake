Heritage Hub Federal Credit Union

Digital Membership Intake — Executive Summary
2645 Winrock Blvd, Houston, TX 77057  •  (832) 941-9122  •  heritagehubfederal.org
ConfidentialPrepared Sept 2026

What was delivered: a secure, mobile-friendly online membership intake form that lets prospective members complete their application and upload supporting documents from any device — replacing the manual, print-and-scan PDF process. It is brand-matched to Heritage Hub FCU and ready to plug into the credit union's website.

1What the Form Does

  • Two applications in one link — a one-click toggle between a Simple (single-owner) membership and a Full business application with beneficial-ownership & BSA details.
  • Complete data capture — business info, owners/control person, authorized signers, eligibility, account selection, beneficiaries, and required disclosures.
  • Built-in guidance & validation — required fields, live progress bar, and formatting for EIN, SSN, phone, and dates reduce incomplete submissions.
  • Document upload (8 categories) — EIN letter, formation/DBA certificate, ID, resolution, and more. Accepts every file type (PDF, HEIC, JPG, Word…).
  • Phone-camera capture — applicants can photograph documents directly, or pick files/photos, or drag-and-drop on desktop.
  • Legally-adopted e-signature — sign by drawing or typing, with an E-SIGN / UETA adoption statement and a captured audit trail.

2Key Capabilities

Any Device
Phone, tablet, or computer; fully responsive.
All File Types
PDF, HEIC, images, Word — up to 25 MB each.
E-Signable
Draw or type; E-SIGN/UETA compliant.
On-Brand
Real HHFCU logo & colors.

3Security & Compliance Posture

  • Designed to send applications only to Heritage Hub FCU's own secure endpoint — sensitive data (SSN, IDs) is not routed through any third-party service.
  • Supports the credit union's BSA / Beneficial Ownership (31 CFR §1010.230) and USA PATRIOT Act obligations; consent, verification, and e-sign audit language are built in.
  • Until connected, the live form runs in a safe review mode that transmits nothing — safe to circulate for review today.
